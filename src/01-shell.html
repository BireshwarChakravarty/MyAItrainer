AI for Procurement & Warehouse Auditing

How to use AI tools to check stock, purchase order and invoice data across several warehouses: what the tools can and can't do, how to ask them for useful work, and how to check what they give you.

14 modules, about 10 hours in total, each with a test and a hands-on assignment. Practice data included. No coding needed.

Who this is for

The course was written for an assistant purchase manager who audits data for seven warehouses, but it works for anyone who checks procurement or inventory records and is new to AI. It starts with the basics and gets more practical as it goes. By the later modules you'll be using AI on the kind of checks you already do: count variances, invoice matching, duplicate payments, split orders and vendor records.

Contents

    How to work through it

    • One module per sitting. Each takes 20 to 45 minutes. Two or three a week gets you through in about a month.
    • Finish each module with its test and assignment. The test checks you understood it (pass mark 70%, retake as often as you like). The assignment has you do the work, mostly on the practice data, with answers checked as you go. A module counts as done when both are complete.
    • Download the practice data before module 2. It's a fictional set of warehouse, purchasing and supplier files with problems planted in it, so you can practise safely in any AI tool.
    • Use the AI tool your company has approved. Everything here works in Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Module 3 covers what data you can and can't share.
    • Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can stop and pick up later on the same device.

    Why bother now

    Most procurement teams now use AI in some form, but very few have made it part of how work actually gets done. EFESO's 2026 survey of procurement leaders put the share that have properly scaled it at about 5%. The most common use is spend analysis. For someone who audits data, the useful skills are the unglamorous ones: asking precise questions, checking the answers, and turning a check that works into a routine. That's what this course concentrates on.
