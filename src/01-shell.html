The course floor plan

Each level builds on the one before it

The course is laid out like a warehouse. Each level is a zone and each module is an aisle. Work through them in order: the early aisles are short and conceptual, the later ones are hands-on and specific to your audit work. Your progress is saved in this browser.

How to get the most out of it

One aisle per sitting

Each module takes 20–45 minutes. Two or three a week is a good pace. You will finish in about a month without it eating your week.

Do the "Try it this week" task

Every module ends with a small real-work task. That is where the learning sticks. Reading alone won't change how you work.

Keep the prompt library open

Over 30 ready-to-use prompts written for procurement and warehouse audit work. Copy, fill in the brackets, use.

Use your company-approved AI tool

Everything here works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Microsoft Copilot. Module 3 explains what data is safe to share and where.

Why this matters now

Procurement teams are adopting AI quickly, but few use it well. Industry surveys in 2025 and 2026 found that most procurement professionals now use generative AI in some form, yet only around 5% of procurement organisations have truly scaled it (EFESO, 2026 CPO Pulse). Spend analysis is the most common use case, cited by 69% of respondents.

For someone who audits data, that gap is an opportunity. The skills in this course (asking good questions of data, checking AI output properly, and turning repeat checks into routines) are the ones that separate people who play with AI from people who get real results from it.
